The Garden After Dark

Moonlit Garden Midnight Café is a greenhouse café that comes alive only after the sun goes down. Sip warm drinks among night-blooming jasmine, glowing lanterns, and fireflies that do the decorating for us.

Garden Features

  • Candlelit greenhouse tables under a glass roof
  • A moonflower walking path
    • Blooms open around 9:00 p.m. each night
    • Lit by solar-charged lanterns
  • Quiet reading benches beside the lily pond

The Night Menu

Visit the Garden

We are open Wednesday through Sunday, 9:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m.
Find us at 305 Martin Luther King Drive, just past the Tuskegee gate. Every 1st Friday of the month we host a stargazing night.

How to Order

  1. Pick a drink or treat from the chalkboard by the gate.
  2. Order at the lantern counter and take a firefly number token.
  3. Collect your order when your token number is called.
